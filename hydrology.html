Domain limits

The entire 65.536 × 65.536 km grid is solved together at 256 m spacing. River flow never resets at a rendering-tile boundary.

Sea-connected outlets and flows reaching the edge of the modeled world are distinguished. A boundary outlet is unresolved beyond the domain, not assumed to join the sea.

Accumulation is contributing area (km²), not a simulated discharge in cubic metres per second. Lake permanence, seasonal rainfall, sediment transport and 3D water are not yet modeled.
