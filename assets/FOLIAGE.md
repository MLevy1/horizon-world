# Woodland foliage asset

`foliage-oak.png` is an original generated alpha-cutout used on intersecting
canopy cards. Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The photograph
provided in discussion was inspected as a woodland-structure reference and was
not incorporated into the texture or redistributed.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: reusable alpha-cutout foliage texture for a real-time 3D woodland canopy, not a scene or concept illustration. Create a single dense irregular cluster of many small temperate oak and beech leaves attached to fine branching twigs, viewed straight on. A roughly round but asymmetric 1.8 metre wide branch spray with layered leaves and convincing small gaps throughout. About 100 individually detailed 4-8 cm leaves, mature mid-green with a few olive and yellow-green leaves. Full natural leaf texture, fine veins and curled edges. Soft neutral diffuse illumination, no directional cast shadow or baked sky light. Fill 85 percent of a square image; entire spray isolated with genuinely transparent background, including gaps between leaves. Crisp cutout alpha edges, no ground, no trunk, no backdrop, no glow, no text, no border. Intended to repeat at different rotations as intersecting foliage cards on branched 3D trees. Do not generate a whole tree or a woodland photograph.

This is a branch-cluster approximation rather than a botanical model. Specific
needle textures, bark assets and fully dynamic cast shadows remain future work.
