HORIZON 0.1.1

A fresh beginning. One deterministic 8 × 8 km test region, with coastlines, terrain, and forest.

Drainage is calculated before the final landscape. The channels are visible on the map, but river water is deliberately not rendered yet. We will add it only after its geometry is validated.

Touch: drag to look, left circle to move, arrows to rise or descend. Desktop: WASD, drag to look, Q/E to fly vertically.

About v0.1

This is intentionally a smaller foundation, not a reconstruction of the old game's features.

World pipeline: broad geography → connected drainage → final ground → rendering and vegetation. The drainage graph does not depend on terrain meshes or camera movement.

The map exposes the computed drainage network before any attempt is made to draw river water. The world is finite by design in this version.

Flight: drag to look; joystick or WASD to move; up/down buttons or E/Q for elevation; Speed button to change between 65 and 330 km/h.
