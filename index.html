Changes are applied to the live procedural landscape.

Compare layered conifers and branched broadleaf silhouettes against the original models. Distant trees use the lightweight originals.

Additional low-poly foliage and branching within about 900 m. Switch off to compare with Alpha 3.1 trees.

Compare deterministic shape variants with Alpha 3.0 trees and shrubs. Detailed variants apply nearby; distant trees remain simple.

Prepare vegetation before approaching it, then dither-fade individual plants in and out. Turn off to compare with Alpha 3.10.

Prioritize terrain ahead of your view and travel direction; adjust small-plant detail for altitude, apparent size and speed. Turn off to compare with Alpha 3.9.

Add clustered grasses, short plants and fern-like foliage on suitable ground close to the camera. Turn off to compare the Alpha 3.2 ground cover.

Alpha 4 shared surface geography is enabled by default. Turn off to compare the original terrain. Waterway routing and independent lakes remain under development.

Alpha 3.19 experimental: select one watershed with Find test waterway. Connected river paths, kilometer-scale headwaters, and irregular depression-backed pond reaches use shared terrain and water profiles. Default off; the earlier river system remains available for comparison.

Compare broader, less repetitive terrain texture against Alpha 3.4 and the original terrain. No extra terrain geometry.

Replace triangular ground-cover spikes with thin blades and branched foliage. Turn off for Alpha 3.4 plant shapes.

Compare visibly darker green and warmer earth patches against the original colors. Uses the same working terrain shader.

Trees and rocks block movement, including in flight. Turn off to pass through scenery.

Draw magenta poles and yellow root dots through the terrain (X-ray view). If a dot is behind the foreground ridge, its tree can look like it floats even though it is rooted.

Center the crosshair directly on the visible foliage. This selects trees by the visible canopy outline rather than unreliable triangle raycasting.
